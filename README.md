## 💫 About Me

I'm a backend architect and engineering leader with **6.5+ years of experience** (since Nov 2019) shipping regulated healthcare software at production scale.

I currently lead the backend team at **[SaveLifeAI](https://savelife.ai)** — a US-based healthcare AI company — where I architect a **23-microservice platform** (Python / FastAPI / Hasura) that powers four clinical products: **RadioViewAI** (Radiology PACS), **AizaMD** (ambient clinical notes), **RadReport** (AI radiology reports), and **ConnectAI** (admin + practice management). The platform serves **1,000+ clinicians globally** and has generated over **1 million medical reports**.

I led the backend delivery of **NeuroICH™**, SaveLifeAI's first FDA 510(k)-cleared product (K241719) — AI brain hemorrhage detection with 97.5% sensitivity — and three additional FDA submissions are currently in flight. I also act as **interim CTO** for strategic engineering decisions.
